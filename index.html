Institutional Overview

About Our College

Dedicated to nurturing technical expertise, research aptitude and ethical professional values.

D. Y. Patil College of Engineering (DYPCOE), located in Akurdi, Pune, was established in 1984 under the aegis of Dr. D. Y. Patil Pratishthan. Situated within the D. Y. Patil Educational Complex in Nigdi Pradhikaran, the college is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi, recognized by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Government of Maharashtra, and affiliated to Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU). The institution holds an 'A' Grade accreditation from NAAC and offers comprehensive degree programs across diverse engineering disciplines.

Teaching & Infrastructure

Academic Environment

The academic framework at DYPCOE aligns with the curriculum set by Savitribai Phule Pune University, combining rigorous theoretical foundations with practical laboratory learning.

  • Qualified faculty members with academic and industry experience
  • Department-specific computing centers and hardware laboratories
  • Central library with access to technical textbooks, reference materials and journals
  • Structured continuous assessment and student mentoring system
Holistic Growth

Learning, Innovation & Development

Beyond standard classroom instruction, students are encouraged to engage in active problem-solving, project-based engineering, and professional skill enhancement.

  • Encouragement for mini-projects, capstone designs and technical competitions
  • Active student chapters of professional bodies and technical clubs
  • Workshops on emerging programming languages, tools and frameworks
  • Guidance for industry internships, seminars and soft-skill development

Admissions

Welcome prospective engineering students. Begin your academic journey at D. Y. Patil College of Engineering, Akurdi, Pune — shaping capable engineers through world-class technical education.

ⓘ

Official Advisory: Engineering admissions are governed through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the Directorate of Technical Education (DTE), Government of Maharashtra (College DTE Code: 6272). Please consult the official DTE/CET Cell Maharashtra portal and college administration office for current admission cut-offs, fee schedules, and required certificates.

Step-by-Step Guide

Your Admission Journey

Follow these key steps to navigate through the engineering admission lifecycle.

01

Explore Programs

Review available engineering branches including Computer Engineering, IT, Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, and Electronics & Telecommunication.

02

Review Eligibility

Understand qualifying entrance examination criteria (MHT-CET / JEE Main) and academic requirements stipulated by Savitribai Phule Pune University and DTE.

03

Submit Application

Register on the state CET Cell CAP portal, upload verified educational documents, and select DYPCOE Akurdi (DTE Code: 6272) in option form preferences.

04

Complete Admission

Upon seat allotment, report to the college campus facilitation center with original documents for verification, fee deposition, and enrollment confirmation.
